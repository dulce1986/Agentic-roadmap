Ranked by Impact

Impact is measured in basis points (bps) — how much each initiative is expected to move the two org metrics: Overall Automation Rate and Automated CSAT. Ranked by the Impact & Ranking Agent; the ranking re-runs whenever evidence or confidence changes.

Score = (automation-rate bps + automated-CSAT bps) × confidence % ÷ effort points, then × priority — P0 ×1.5 · P1 ×1.25 · P2 ×1.0. Higher score ranks first.

How to read this

A request arrives in Intake (Slack, Linear or a form). The Intake & Routing Agent cleans it and sends it to the right team in Routing. The Evidence Agent attaches proof, the Impact Agent scores and ranks it, then it is Placed on a Big Rock and timeline. Delivery tracks it in Linear, and the Digest turns its progress into plain-English updates. Nothing skips a stage — that is what makes the roadmap trustworthy.
